Roundup 01 · Clutter

15 desk accessories that actually cut clutter

Clutter is a systems problem, not a storage problem. After wiring up more messy desks than I can count as an engineer, here are the 15 pieces that fixed mine — grouped by the job they do, each with the tradeoff so you don't buy the wrong one.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Some links below are affiliate links — you pay the same price, and it helps keep these reviews independent. I only list gear I'd put on my own desk.

Surface organizers

Get things off the desktop and into a home. Footprint is the whole game here.

01

Bamboo monitor stand with drawer

Lifts the screen to eye level and swallows pens, cables, and a phone in the drawer underneath — two problems, one footprint.

Tradeoff The drawer is shallow; a full mouse won't fit flat.

02

Under-desk headphone hook

Adhesive hook that reclaims the surface your headset used to eat. Sticks under the lip, invisible from the front.

Tradeoff Adhesive needs a clean, non-textured surface to hold.

03

Slim pen and tool tray

A single low tray beats a cup — you see everything at a glance and it doesn't tip.

Tradeoff Holds less than a cup; forces you to keep only what you use.

Cable control

The single biggest visual-clutter win. Do this group first if you do nothing else.

04

Under-desk cable tray

Everything — power strip, charger bricks, loose cable — lifts off the floor into one hidden channel. This is the piece that makes a desk look "done."

Tradeoff Clamp-on models need a desk edge under ~4cm thick.

05

Adhesive cable clips

Route the one or two cables that have to reach the desktop so they stop sliding behind the desk every time you unplug.

Tradeoff The adhesive is one-and-done — reposition once and it won't re-stick.

06

Cable sleeve wrap

Bundles the runs you can't hide into one clean tube. Cheapest upgrade on this list per dollar of visible improvement.

Tradeoff Fiddly to add or remove a cable later — set it once.

Screen and light

Clears the desktop of lamps and second-monitor sprawl while you're at it.

07

Single monitor arm

Frees the entire footprint under your screen and lets you push the monitor back for more desk. The upgrade people notice most.

Tradeoff Check your desk depth and VESA mount before buying.

08

Monitor light bar

Clips to the top of the screen and lights the desk without a lamp taking up surface or throwing glare.

Tradeoff Needs a USB port you may already have spoken for.

If you only buy three

  1. Start with the under-desk cable tray — biggest visual change per dollar.
  2. Add the monitor arm to reclaim the desktop under your screen.
  3. Finish with the bamboo stand with drawer if you skipped the arm, or the light bar if you didn't.
