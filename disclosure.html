Affiliate disclosure

Desk Atlas is reader-supported. Here's exactly how that works, in plain terms.

The short version

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Some links on this site are affiliate links: if you click one and buy something, I may earn a small commission. You pay the same price either way — the commission comes out of the retailer's margin, not your pocket.

What this doesn't change

I only recommend gear I would put on my own desk, and every pick lists its tradeoff, not just its upside. A commission never buys a spot on a list or a better verdict. When something's mediocre, I say so or leave it off.

Beyond Amazon

Some links go to other retailers or brands with their own affiliate programs. The same rule applies: same price for you, honest verdict from me.

Questions about how a product was chosen? That's fair — reach out any time and I'll tell you straight.
