Quick answers

Desk setup questions, answered.

What should I upgrade first?

Start with the problem you notice every day: cable control for visual clutter, monitor position for discomfort, or lighting for a dim workspace.

What makes a desk feel premium?

Proportion, materials, light and restraint. A premium setup removes friction before it adds objects.
